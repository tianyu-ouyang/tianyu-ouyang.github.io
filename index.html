Welcome to My Homepage!

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Finance at Macquarie University. My doctoral research focuses on stablecoins, digital finance, and financial regulation, with particular interest in how regulatory developments and market dynamics shape the stability, liquidity, and governance of digital currencies.

More broadly, my research interests concern the transformation of financial markets under technological change and the interaction between public policy, financial innovation, and institutional development.
